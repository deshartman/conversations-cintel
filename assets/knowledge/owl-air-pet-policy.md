# Owl Air Pet Travel Policy

Source type: Demo knowledge base article  
Audience: Owl Air customer service AI agents  
Applies to: Owl Air-operated flights  
Last reviewed: 2026-08-04

## Quick Answer

Owl Air accepts small domestic cats and dogs in the cabin on eligible flights when they travel in
an approved carrier under the seat. Each passenger may bring 1 pet carrier, and the carrier counts
as the passenger's carry-on bag. The combined weight of the pet and carrier must not exceed 20 lb /
9 kg.

Pets are not accepted in the cabin on Owl Air flights between London and San Francisco. For flights
OA404 and OA500, cats and dogs may travel only through Owl Air Pet Cargo, subject to advance
approval and the entry requirements of the destination country. Trained service dogs follow the
separate service-animal rules below.

## Animals Owl Air Accepts

Owl Air accepts these animals as pets:

- Domestic cats.
- Domestic dogs.

Owl Air does not accept the following animals as cabin pets or checked pets:

- Birds, including pet owls and parrots.
- Rabbits, rodents, reptiles, amphibians, fish, and insects.
- Farm animals or livestock.
- Exotic or wild animals.
- Venomous animals.

Customers asking to transport an animal that is not a domestic cat or dog should contact a
specialist animal-transport company. The agent must not promise that Owl Air can transport it.

## Pets in the Cabin

Cabin pet travel is available only on eligible flights and requires advance confirmation.

- The pet must be a domestic cat or dog at least 12 weeks old.
- The pet must remain inside its closed carrier at the airport and throughout the flight.
- The carrier must fit completely under the seat in front of the passenger.
- The maximum carrier size is 18 x 11 x 11 in / 46 x 28 x 28 cm.
- The combined weight of the pet and carrier must not exceed 20 lb / 9 kg.
- Only 1 pet is permitted in each carrier.
- Each passenger may travel with no more than 1 pet carrier.
- A pet carrier counts as the passenger's carry-on bag. The passenger may also bring 1 personal
  item.
- Pets may not occupy a passenger seat or travel in an overhead bin.
- Passengers travelling with pets may not sit in an exit row or bulkhead row.

Cabin pet space is limited. The agent should say that the pet is eligible to travel, not that space
is confirmed, unless a booking tool confirms the pet reservation.

## Pet Travel Fees

The demo cabin-pet fee is $125 each way for eligible flights within the United States. Fees for Pet
Cargo and international travel depend on the route, animal, carrier, and required handling.

The agent may provide the $125 domestic cabin estimate but must not quote or promise an
international Pet Cargo price without confirmation from the specialist desk.

## London to San Francisco Flights

Pets are not accepted in the cabin on Owl Air flights between London Heathrow and San Francisco.
This restriction applies to cancelled flight OA404 and replacement flight OA500.

For OA404 or OA500:

- Domestic cats and dogs may be eligible to travel through Owl Air Pet Cargo.
- Pet Cargo must be arranged before travel and is not added as ordinary checked baggage.
- The customer is responsible for required health certificates, vaccinations, microchip records,
  permits, and destination-entry documents.
- The customer should confirm current United Kingdom export and United States entry requirements
  before travel.
- Owl Air cannot guarantee acceptance until the Pet Cargo team reviews the animal, carrier,
  documents, route, and weather conditions.

If customer asks whether a cat or dog can travel on replacement flight OA500, explain that the
pet cannot travel in the cabin and offer to connect customer with the Pet Cargo team.

## Checked Pets and Pet Cargo

Owl Air does not accept pets as ordinary checked baggage. When available, eligible cats and dogs
travel through Owl Air Pet Cargo in a compliant hard-sided kennel.

Pet Cargo acceptance depends on:

- Aircraft and route restrictions.
- Safe weather conditions at the origin, connection points, and destination.
- The animal's age, health, and breed.
- Kennel size, construction, ventilation, and condition.
- Complete health and travel documentation.

The Pet Cargo team makes the final acceptance decision. Agents must not describe a Pet Cargo
request as confirmed until the specialist team approves it.

## Trained Service Dogs

Owl Air accepts trained service dogs that are individually trained to perform work or tasks for a
passenger with a disability, subject to applicable documentation and safety requirements.

- A trained service dog does not count as a cabin pet.
- The dog must remain under the handler's control and fit safely within the handler's foot space.
- The dog may not occupy a seat, block an aisle, or extend into another passenger's space.
- Required service-animal and destination-entry forms must be completed before travel.
- For international travel, animal health and entry requirements still apply.

Emotional-support, comfort, and therapy animals that are not trained service dogs travel under the
standard pet policy. Describing an animal as emotional support does not make it a service animal.

## Health and Safety Restrictions

Owl Air may refuse pet travel when conditions could endanger the animal, passengers, or crew.
Examples include an animal showing aggressive behavior, an unsuitable carrier, extreme weather,
missing documentation, or a destination restriction.

Sedation is discouraged because it can create health risks during air travel. Customers should
discuss medication and fitness to fly with a veterinarian. Owl Air agents must not provide
veterinary advice.

## Agent Guidance

When a customer asks, "Can I bring my pet?", ask these questions if the answers are not already
known:

1. What kind of animal is it?
2. What is the flight number or route?
3. Is the animal a trained service dog?
4. Does the customer want the animal in the cabin or transported through Pet Cargo?
5. What is the combined weight of the pet and carrier?

Give the direct eligibility answer first. Then explain the most important restriction and the next
step. Do not say that a pet reservation is confirmed unless a booking or specialist tool confirms
it.

## Example Customer Questions

Question: "Can I bring my cat?"

Answer: "Small domestic cats can travel in the cabin on eligible Owl Air flights if the cat and
carrier weigh no more than 20 pounds, or 9 kilograms. What is your flight number or route?"

Question: "Can I bring my dog on OA500 from London to San Francisco?"

Answer: "Dogs cannot travel in the cabin on OA500. Your dog may be eligible to travel through Owl
Air Pet Cargo with advance approval and the required international health and entry documents."

Question: "Can I bring my rabbit?"

Answer: "Owl Air accepts only domestic cats and dogs as pets, so rabbits cannot travel in the cabin
or through Owl Air Pet Cargo."

Question: "Can I bring my emotional-support dog?"

Answer: "An emotional-support dog that is not a trained service dog follows the standard pet
policy. It may travel as a cabin pet on an eligible flight if it meets the carrier and weight rules."

Question: "Can I bring my trained service dog to San Francisco?"

Answer: "Trained service dogs may travel in the cabin when the required forms, health documents,
and destination-entry requirements are complete. The dog must remain under your control and fit
safely within your foot space."

Question: "Can I bring my pet owl on Owl Air?"

Answer: "Owl Air accepts only domestic cats and dogs as pets, so birds, including pet owls, cannot
travel in the cabin or through Owl Air Pet Cargo."
