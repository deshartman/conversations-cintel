# Owl Air Luggage Policy

Source type: Demo knowledge base article
Audience: Owl Air customer service AI agents
Applies to: Owl Air-operated flights
Last reviewed: 2026-08-03

## Quick Answer

For most Owl Air economy tickets, each passenger may bring 1 personal item, 1 carry-on bag, and 1 checked bag. Owl Club Gold members may bring 2 checked bags at no extra charge. Checked bags must weigh 50 lb / 23 kg or less unless the passenger has an Owl Club Gold or Business Flex allowance, which permits bags up to 70 lb / 32 kg.

If the customer asks, "What is the baggage policy for my flight?", answer using their booking and loyalty tier when available. For Avery Stone on flight OA404, Avery is an Owl Club Gold member, so she may bring 1 personal item, 1 carry-on bag, and 2 checked bags at no extra charge, up to 70 lb / 32 kg each.

## Carry-On Bags

Every Owl Air passenger may bring the following items into the cabin:

- 1 personal item that fits under the seat in front of them.
- 1 carry-on bag that fits in the overhead bin.

Personal items include small backpacks, purses, laptop bags, briefcases, and medical or mobility items. A personal item should be no larger than 18 x 14 x 8 in / 45 x 35 x 20 cm.

Carry-on bags should be no larger than 22 x 14 x 9 in / 56 x 36 x 23 cm, including wheels and handles. If overhead bin space is limited, Owl Air may gate-check carry-on bags at no charge.

Assistive devices, medical devices, child safety seats, and a small diaper bag do not count against the standard carry-on allowance.

## Checked Bag Allowance

Standard checked bag allowance depends on ticket type and loyalty status:

| Customer type | Included checked bags | Maximum weight per bag |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Economy Saver | 0 | 50 lb / 23 kg |
| Economy Standard | 1 | 50 lb / 23 kg |
| Economy Flex | 1 | 50 lb / 23 kg |
| Business Flex | 2 | 70 lb / 32 kg |
| Owl Club Silver | 1 | 50 lb / 23 kg |
| Owl Club Gold | 2 | 70 lb / 32 kg |

When a passenger qualifies for more than one allowance, use the most generous allowance. For example, an Owl Club Gold member on an Economy Standard ticket receives the Owl Club Gold allowance of 2 checked bags up to 70 lb / 32 kg each.

Checked bags should be no larger than 62 linear inches / 158 linear cm when adding length + width + height.

## Extra, Overweight, and Oversized Bags

Customers may add extra checked bags when space is available. Demo agents should describe fees generally unless the exact fee is present in a booking or payment system.

Use these demo fee ranges when a customer asks for an estimate:

| Bag type | Demo fee estimate |
| --- | ---: |
| First checked bag when not included | $35 |
| Second checked bag when not included | $45 |
| Third or additional checked bag | $125 each |
| Overweight bag from 51-70 lb / 24-32 kg | $100 |
| Oversized bag over 62 linear in / 158 linear cm | $150 |

Bags over 70 lb / 32 kg, or over 80 linear in / 203 linear cm, may need to travel as cargo and should not be promised as standard checked baggage.

## Sports Equipment and Special Items

Sports equipment can usually be checked if it is packed safely and meets the checked bag size and weight limits. Golf clubs, skis, snowboards, and fishing equipment count as one checked bag when packed in a suitable case.

Musical instruments may travel as carry-on items if they fit in the overhead bin or under the seat. Larger instruments require a purchased seat or checked baggage handling, depending on size.

Lithium batteries, spare power banks, e-cigarettes, and vaping devices must travel in carry-on baggage and must not be packed in checked bags.

## Restricted Items

Passengers may not pack hazardous materials in checked or carry-on baggage. This includes explosives, fireworks, compressed gas cylinders, flammable liquids, and corrosive chemicals.

Small toiletries and liquids in carry-on bags must follow airport security rules. For flights departing the United Kingdom, customers should follow current airport liquid screening instructions at the departure airport.

If a customer asks whether a specific item is allowed and the answer is safety-sensitive or unclear, advise them to check the airport security guidance and Owl Air's special items desk before travel.

## Lost, Delayed, or Damaged Bags

If a checked bag is delayed, the customer should file a baggage report before leaving the airport or contact Owl Air support as soon as possible. The agent should collect the passenger name, flight number, bag tag number if available, delivery address, and a callback number.

For delayed baggage in the demo, the agent may offer a concise apology and explain the next step. Do not promise compensation unless a tool or supervisor policy confirms eligibility.

If a bag is damaged, the customer should keep the bag, photos, receipts, and bag tag until the claim is reviewed.

## Agent Guidance

- Prefer a short, direct answer first, then ask whether they want help adding a bag or checking a special item.
- Do not invent exact fees beyond the demo estimates in this article.
- Do not say that baggage has been added, waived, refunded, or compensated unless a tool confirms the action.
- If the customer is Avery Stone, use Owl Club Gold baggage benefits in the answer.
